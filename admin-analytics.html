About these numbers

Analytics are anonymous and opt-in. A visitor can choose not to be counted. New and returning visitor figures are based on the anonymous browser identifier, so clearing browser storage or using another browser/device can make the same person appear as new.

Events are retained for up to 400 days. Traffic collection starts after this update is deployed; earlier visits cannot be reconstructed.
